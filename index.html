Posefie 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 9 月 17 日

Posefie 不收集你的任何数据。没有账号，没有网络请求，没有第三方 SDK，没有广告，没有分析工具。

我们收集什么

不收集任何信息。App 内没有任何一行代码会把数据发送到我们或任何第三方的服务器——它根本不访问网络。

你的照片在哪里

  • 你拍的照片保存到你自己的系统相册，和用系统相机拍的照片一样。
  • 「挑一张」用的缩略图保存在本机的缓存目录里，只保留最近几张，系统内存紧张时会自动清理。
  • 你设置的角度、留白、焦距等参数保存在本机。
  • 分享海报在本机生成，放在 App 的临时目录里，下次分享时覆盖；发给谁，由你在系统分享面板里决定。如果要用的那张原图当时只存在 iCloud 上，系统会替你把它取回本机——那是 iOS 自己的照片同步，不经过我们（我们没有服务器）。

以上数据都不会离开你的设备。卸载 App 即全部删除（已保存到系统相册的照片属于你的相册，卸载不会动它们）。

权限用途

  • 相机：作为取景器显示画面并拍照。这是 App 的核心功能。
  • 相册（写入）：把你拍的照片保存到相册。
  • 相册（读写）：用于四件事——保存照片；在你确认后删除你主动选择不要的那几张（删除会经过系统确认弹窗，照片进入「最近删除」，30 天内可恢复）；检查「挑一张」里列出的照片是否还在相册中；你点分享时，读取你自己拍的那一张原图，用来在本机生成海报。不读取、不上传你相册里的其它照片。
  • 从海报导入（不需要权限）：选图走系统的照片选择器，整个过程在系统的独立进程里完成，只有你亲手点中的那一张会递给 App。这条路上 App 看不到相册里的其它照片，也不会为此申请相册读取权限。

儿童

本 App 不收集任何数据，因此也不会收集儿童的任何信息。

Posefie Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Posefie collects nothing. No account, no network requests, no third-party SDKs, no ads, no analytics.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no code in this app that sends data to us or to any third party — the app does not access the network at all.

Where your photos live

  • Photos you take are saved to your own system photo library, exactly as they would be with the built-in Camera app.
  • Thumbnails used by the review screen are stored in the app's local cache, limited to the most recent shots, and are cleared automatically by the system when storage is tight.
  • The angles, headroom, focal length and other settings you save are stored on the device.
  • Share posters are rendered on the device into the app's temporary directory and overwritten the next time you share; where one goes is your choice in the system share sheet. If the original happens to live only in iCloud at that moment, the system fetches it back for you — that is iOS's own photo syncing, and it does not go through us (we have no servers).

None of this leaves your device. Deleting the app removes all of it (photos already saved to your system photo library belong to your library and are not touched).

Permissions

  • Camera: to show the viewfinder and take photos. This is the app's core function.
  • Photo library (add): to save the photos you take.
  • Photo library (read/write): for four things — saving photos; deleting the shots you explicitly choose to discard, after you confirm in the system dialog (deleted photos go to Recently Deleted and stay recoverable for 30 days); checking whether the photos listed in the review screen are still present in your library; and, when you tap share, reading the original of that one photo you took so the poster can be rendered on the device. It does not read or upload any other photos in your library.
  • Importing from a poster (no permission needed): picking the image goes through the system photo picker, which runs in its own process — only the single photo you tap is handed to the app. The app never sees any other photo in your library along this path, and asks for no photo-library read access for it.

Children

This app collects no data, and therefore collects no information from children.
